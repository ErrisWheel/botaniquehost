Our History

This vision started with a simple love for plants and nature. We wanted to help people bring more greenery and beauty into their homes.

What started as a small idea became a plant shop that offers different plants and products for plant lovers. We want to make it easy and enjoyable for everyone to care for plants.

As our business grew, we continued to add more plants and improve our services. We also built a community of people who enjoy plants and nature.

Today, BOTANIQUE is more than just a plant shop. We are here to help people make their spaces greener, fresher, and more beautiful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

How can I place an order?

You can place an order directly through our website. Browse our collection of plants, select your desired product, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout to complete your purchase.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept Cash and Maya as payment methods for your convenience.

Do you offer international shipping?

At the moment, we only offer local delivery due to the limited availability of courier services for international shipments. However, we look forward to expanding our services and serving international customers in the future.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Yes! Your satisfaction is our priority. If you wish to cancel or modify your order, please contact us as soon as possible. Our team will be happy to assist you with your request.

How can I track my order?

Visit your account's order history to check the status of your purchase. If you need additional assistance, you can contact our team through the Contact Us page.

What should I do if my plant arrives damaged?

Please contact our customer support through the Contact Us page as soon as possible. Provide details about the issue so we can review your concern and assist you accordingly.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes 3–5 days after placing your order. However, the actual delivery time may vary depending on your location, order processing time, and courier availability.

How can I contact Botanique?

You can visit our Contact Us page to send us your questions, concerns, or feedback. We are happy to hear from you and will do our best to assist you.
